## 🔍 Key Findings & Business Insights

1. **Demographic Revenue Concentration:**
   * The **25–34 age segment** is the primary commercial driver, contributing **41.0%** of total revenue.
   * Combined with the **35–44 cohort (18.2%)** and **18–24 cohort (17.7%)**, shoppers aged 18–44 generate **~77%** of total store revenue.
   * Older demographics (55+) represent less than 12% of revenue combined, suggesting digital marketing campaigns should heavily target young professionals.

2. **Traffic Volatility & Campaign Spikes:**
   * Web traffic and revenue exhibit distinct surge periods (notably around **June 10 and June 24–25**), where sessions peaked above 12K–14K daily visits.
   * Landing page analysis highlights `Home`, `New | Google Merch`, and `Google Merch Shop` as the critical high-traffic entry funnels.

3. **Dashboard Controls & Dimensional Filtering:**
   * Integrated dynamic drop-downs allowing stakeholders to slice data by **Session Default Channel Group**, **Country**, **Ad Format**, and **Device Category**.
